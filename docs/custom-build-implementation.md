# Oak's Draft: Custom Build V3 — playable test build

Start a **new game**. On PLAY STYLE, press SELECT until **CUSTOM BUILD** appears (after the original OAK'S DRAFT). Choose difficulty, optional Nuzlocke and MGM. The original Last Six mode stays separately selectable. Use in-game saves when changing builds; do not carry emulator save states between ROM revisions.

## Agreed rules implemented

- Six crates each contain an earliest-stage evolving candidate, a non-Mega single-stage candidate and a Mega-capable single-stage candidate. Supported official regional forms, Legendaries, Mythicals, Ultra Beasts and Paradox species are eligible when their category fits. Evolutionary families do not repeat across the 18 candidates. Temporary and unsupported forms are excluded.
- Before committing, compare sprite, species, types, current randomized BST, category, remaining normal stages and Mega eligibility. Future potential, ability choices, moves and floors stay hidden. Choose your own nickname through the native naming screen.
- Final normal potential rolls uniformly within the feasible part of 300–700, conditional on a minimum starting BST of 180. Normal evolution differences are reserved backward, with at least 10 points per evolution. Branches share one final potential: the largest remaining weighted path is reserved and a shorter branch catches up deterministically. No potential rerolls during evolution.
- Single-stage Mega candidates roll their Mega endpoint independently, then subtract a 40–160 point Mega reward. Their starting BST is lower on average than the immediate-power category. Evolving lines can also earn a 40–160 Mega reward after their normal final stage; their Mega total may exceed 700.
- Three distinct valid abilities have fully paged descriptions. After normal/permanent Mega evolution, choose the current ability or one of two new abilities. The existing paid one-time Game Corner reroll updates the individual build too.
- Four random starting moves are revealed after commitment, including one usable damaging move. Other valid moves can include high-power, OHKO and self-sacrifice moves; there is no extra power cap. Existing seeded randomized level-up/relearner behavior continues. Z/Max moves cannot enter the pool.
- Independent minimum floors use the specified BST tiers. Minimums are automatically allocated and trimmed within their tier if necessary to reserve at least 25% for manual allocation. Confirmed allocations are permanent. Six **16-bit individual base stats** support values above 255, within the safe total budget, without altering species tables. IVs, EVs, level and nature still apply normally.
- Allocation controls: Up/Down select a stat; Left/Right adjust one; L/R adjust ten; SELECT resets the unconfirmed allocation to its floors; START reviews after all points are spent. A confirms twice. Evolution preserves every prior point and offers only newly earned points for allocation.
- Permanent Mega use: own the existing Mega Ring and matching stone, then use the stone from the bag on the eligible companion. Rayquaza uses the Ring with Dragon Ascent. Existing acquisition gates and one active party Mega remain. Stones are not consumed; normal held items remain usable. Authored opponent Mega battles remain enabled.
- Gifts and the nine ordinary Kanto NPC trades use the Custom Build inspection/build workflow. Eggs and Game Corner Pokémon acquisition remain excluded. A living current companion is permanently surrendered for one level-matched gift. The preview reveals species/types/current BST only. Ability/moves/floors are revealed during building. Final cancellation preserves the entire roster. Held items are returned before the atomic swap; a full bag prevents replacement. Nuzlocke vacancies cannot be filled by gifts.
- Visible and hidden item pickups use stable per-location mappings. The optional Memorial Pillar reward explicitly opts in, including its full-bag retry. Keys, HMs, fossils and required Mega sources remain protected. Ordinary shops, repeatable reward economies and unrelated NPC rewards remain unchanged.
- Gym TMs use actual current-party learnability, favor useful unknown coverage and avoid previously acquired/rewarded TMs where possible. Issued Gym TMs are excluded from later external sources, including shops and item pools. When every new compatible TM is exhausted, a compatible previously seen non-Gym TM is the first fallback; if no distinct compatible Gym reward exists, the scripted safe fallback is a Rare Candy. Badges/story rewards still proceed, with actual TM/move details shown.
- Rival chooses the stronger current-BST rejected candidate and announces it. Oak takes the other without announcing a future team. NPC builds are independently and deterministically allocated, retain draft personalities and species families, evolve along existing progression, receive level-appropriate moves/items and can develop one permanent Mega at late levels. Oak remains the Draft Champion; later story changes are outside this build.
- Capture/free acquisition restrictions, six-on-six opening, post-battle healing, normal difficulty healing and optional Nuzlocke grave handling remain. The opening rival battle is exempt from permadeath. Field HMs continue to depend on badge/item access, not party learnsets. Full party Gym access remains as approved.

## Global removal and existing saves

Dynamax/Gigantamax, Terastallization and Z-Move activation are denied for both sides. Associated items cannot be newly awarded by shared item routing or generated by the IronMon pools; legacy bag items are inert, legacy held items read as empty, and stored Z/Max move IDs resolve to Tackle. Related Tera-only abilities are excluded from randomized ability selection. Permanent Mega systems remain.

SaveBlock1/2/3, BoxPokemon and Pokemon sizes and all existing offsets are unchanged. Six packed 26-byte individual records use deprecated Dex flag buffers that have no other readers/writers in this tree. Records survive party order, storage, healing, evolution and flash save/load. NPC profiles are reconstructed from the seed and personality. New Custom runs use starter mode 5; the original Draft remains mode 4. Future releases must preserve this generator or provide migration before changing candidate/potential generation. Cross-ROM link transfers of custom records are not supported; Draft link-trade entry remains blocked.

## Validation and practical limits

The ROM compiles with the project's FireRed target. Native tests use mGBA 0.10.2 at 240×160, real screens/scripts/evolution animations and actual flash writes. Battle completion fixtures control HP, status and moves to validate progression; they do not demonstrate balance. Source and native results are stored beside this document.

This is a playable **test build**, not a claim of a full natural playthrough or testing on physical iOS Delta. Those remain outstanding. Rival selection uses current BST rather than a coverage optimizer. NPC allocations are deterministic balanced builds rather than a competitive team search. Only explicitly safe optional exploration rewards opt into randomization; generic quest payouts are preserved. Gift swap atomicity is an in-memory transaction before normal game saving; resetting before an in-game save restores the earlier saved run. Native emulator fixtures must restore matching flash journals with RAM to avoid testing a different save slot by accident.

The earlier fixes for the rival aisle route, complete bookcase art, branch text paging, empty post-battle starter balls and draft-room exit remain included. Tracker skin/run-counter work is separate and not changed by this ROM.
