# Pokémon availability plan

Status: planned; species coverage has not yet been guaranteed or audited.

- Make every standard Pokémon species obtainable in Chaos. Evolution can cover later stages; wild encounters are one source rather than the entire roster.
- Keep unfiltered Random and Scaled encounter tables at their native species counts, preserving repeated weighted slots and native levels.
- Keep existing type and ability filter rules for now; review their availability rules separately.
- Use Silph Co. gifts, fossil revivals, Game Corner prizes, other existing gifts/statics, and additional static events to cover species absent from wild pools.
- Audit both the normal encounter roster and individual randomized seeds. Independent random rolls alone cannot guarantee full coverage in each seed.
- Preserve the policy that personal custom species enter random wild pools only when explicitly approved.

Before implementation, create a species-to-source checklist identifying each species' wild, evolution, gift, fossil, prize, or static source, and any progression requirements.
