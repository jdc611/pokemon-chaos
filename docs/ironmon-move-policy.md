# IronMON move eligibility

Both IronMON modes use the same policy in `IronmonMoveAllowed`. Destiny Bond is excluded from generated learnsets. Empty, invalid, zero-PP entries and Struggle are also excluded from normal learnset generation; automatic battle Struggle remains available.

Perish Song, all four OHKO moves, Explosion, Self-Destruct, Misty Explosion, Memento, Healing Wish, Lunar Dance and Final Gambit remain eligible. These moves use the existing engine restrictions, accuracy, targeting, immunities, priority and faint handling. They are not silently removed from opponent generation. Self-sacrifice is a losing choice for the sole player main, and can also leave an opponent down a Pokémon. OHKO moves retain their level/accuracy restrictions. Perish Song and Perish Body retain their visible countdown: the solo main must finish the battle before its countdown expires. Contact choice and completing the fight remain relevant, but unfavorable ability/move combinations can end a run. No seed is guaranteed winnable. Trapping and unusual immunity combinations retain ordinary battle mechanics.

This deliberately narrow decision preserves ordinary strategic risk rather than applying the candidate list as a blanket ban. It does not assert that every possible ability/move combination has been exhaustively simulated.

The starter's guaranteed damaging move additionally excludes self-sacrifice, attacks that require a specific opponent action/status, and attacks with special prerequisite conditions. This selection rule only ensures one functional attack; it does not remove those moves from general learnsets, promise strong coverage, or reroll a bad starter.

Healing and draining moves remain legal. Legitimate held-item and ability recovery remain legal. IronMON skips the normal battle-end held-consumable refund. Unlimited legitimate randomized level-up relearning remains available, but replacing a move carries its remaining PP, clamped to the new maximum. The free Move Deleter is unavailable to prevent deleting then relearning an empty slot for full PP. Newly learned moves in genuine empty slots retain normal learning behavior. PP Up/PP Max cannot restore PP through field use. Normal Chaos keeps its existing behavior.

The Vs. Seeker is unavailable in IronMON to prevent repeatable rematch grinding. One-time story and Mega Stone rematches remain accessible through their existing progression events. Trainer Tower and visiting-trainer battles retain the existing facility rule of awarding no EXP. Trainer Tower templates have no authored levels and ordinarily scale to the player's party; IronMON instead uses fixed level 50, seeded ordinary generation, MGM and Singles. This optional postgame balancing value is provisional for playtesting.
